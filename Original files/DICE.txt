DICE
Hero 


Hero dice is stronger than Class dice, it has 6 faces each with a different combination of attributes based on which Hero they belong to.


1. Warrior  : squares primary, triangles secondary, circles tertiary
2. Sentinel : squares primary, circles secondary, triangles tertiary
3. Mage     : circles primary, triangles secondary, squares tertiary
4. Shaman : circles primary, squares secondary, triangles tertiary
5. Thief      : triangles primary, circles secondary, squares tertiary
6. Hunter   : triangles primary, squares secondary, circles tertiary
7. Mercenary has unique dice


Faces’s attributes for everybody but mercenary:


1. 2 primary + 1 secondary
2. 1 primary + 2 secondary
3. 1 primary + 1 secondary
4. 2 primary 
5. 1 primary 
6. 1 tertiary 


Mercenary's dice faces’s attributes:


1. 2 squares 
2. 2 circles
3. 2 triangles
4. 1 square + 1 circle
5. 1 circle + 1 triangle
6. 1 triangle + 1 square
