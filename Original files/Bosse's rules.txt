Rank 1


1. Every time a monster is killed add 1 G to this boss reward
2. This boss has 1 more HoB
3. Players can only use 1 spell per turn
4. This boss deals one damage to the attacker every time one of his hearths is taken


Rank 2


1. This boss gains + 1 A for every Charm in the game
2. This boss can only be fought Coop
3. Everything cost 1 more G this stage
4. Monsters have +1 A this stage


Rank 3


1. Every time a player loses a life in combat add one marker to this boss’s card, at 2+(number of players) markers players lose the game
2. Every time players buy equipment add one marker to this boss’s card, at 6+(number of players x2) markers players lose the game
3. Players who die cannot revive this stage, every 3 turns all players lose 1 health on p 5, if all players die the game is lost
4. Every 3 turns on p 5 all players have to pay 2 G to the Boss, if a player can’t pay they die and are out of the game
